How it works
One platform. Two ways you get paid.

You earn on every completed booking as the platform owner, and you also collect your full rate when clients book you as the featured therapist.

For clients
Step 1–3
  • Browse therapists by location, style & availability.
  • Book & pay through the app with card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.
  • Relax at home while the therapist comes to them—no driving, no spa wait room.
For you & therapists
Owner earnings
  • Owner fee per booking (ex. 20–25%) is automatically kept by the platform.
  • Therapists get paid out to their bank accounts on a schedule you set.
  • You as featured therapist: when clients choose you, you keep your full rate and the platform fee.

Questions
How do payments and earnings work?

The real app will use Stripe (or similar) to split payments automatically: client pays → your platform takes a fee → the therapist gets paid out.

How do I get paid as the owner? You collect a platform fee (for example 20–25%) from every completed booking. When a client books you personally, you keep both your therapist rate and the platform fee.
Can I let other therapists join? Yes. Each therapist gets a profile, sets their availability, and accepts bookings. The app handles scheduling and payments, so you can scale beyond just your own time.
Do I need the full mobile app right away? No. You can launch with this website, a booking form, and payment links first. Once you’re getting real bookings, you invest in the native iOS/Android app to automate everything.
