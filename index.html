How it works

Simple, human, and straightforward—no complicated tech. Use this process now, then later you can connect a booking app or payment system if you want.

Step 1 · Reach out
  • Call, text, or send a message through the form below.
  • Tell me what you’re dealing with (stress, pain, tight areas, etc.).
  • We’ll confirm if mobile massage is a good fit for you.
Step 2 · Confirm time & place
  • We choose a day and time that works for you.
  • You get a simple confirmation message with what to expect.
  • I arrive a few minutes early to set up the table and create a calm space.
Step 3 · Enjoy your session
  • We talk briefly about pressure, focus areas, and any injuries.
  • You relax while I handle the rest.
  • After the session, I’ll share simple tips to keep your body feeling good.
Step 4 · Pay & plan ahead
  • You can pay via cash, card, or app—whatever you choose to accept.
  • If you like, we set up a regular schedule (every 2–4 weeks).
  • In the future, this can all be automated through your own booking app.
